## High-Fidelity Prototype: Custom Fragrance E-commerce Platform

#Overview

This repository documents the design and prototyping process for a custom fragrance e-commerce platform, developed as part of an academic assignment. The project follows a structured design methodology, progressing from low-fidelity wireframes to a high-fidelity, interactive prototype using Figma.

The platform is designed to guide users through a personalized "Fragrance Journey," allowing them to create and purchase their own unique perfume based on their preferences.

Key Features of the Design

Based on the visual analysis of the prototype, the design includes the following key sections and functionalities:

•
Personalized Creation Process: A multi-step process that guides the user through selecting fragrance notes, bottle style, and quantity.

•
User Authentication: Dedicated screens for Sign-Up and Sign-In.

•
Product Showcase: Sections to display pre-made or featured perfumes.

•
Informational Content: Sections detailing the "Creation Process," "How It All Comes to Life," and "Our Values."

•
Dark Mode Aesthetic: The design utilizes a sophisticated dark background with purple accents, suggesting a premium and mystique brand identity.

Design Methodology

The project followed a multi-stage prototyping methodology as outlined in the assignment brief:

1. Low-Fidelity Wireframes: Initial structural design and layout.

2. Mid-Fidelity Mock-ups: Addition of visual elements, color, and typography.

3. High-Fidelity Prototype: Final design with interactive elements, animations, and detailed visual assets.

The process involved multiple iterations and incorporated end-user feedback at each stage to refine the design and ensure usability, as detailed in the accompanying report.
